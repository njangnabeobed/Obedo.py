About SMASCO

A place to learn, grow and achieve.

St. Mary's Seminary Senior High School (SMASCO) is located at Lolobi in the Oti Region of Ghana. The school is described in the original website as a Category B, single-sex school.

SMASCO aims to provide students with an environment where they can develop their knowledge, talents, discipline, and confidence.
